A: Document Style

Ultra-clean, resume-like. Subtle section dividers with lines. Uppercase small labels for section titles. Very close to Laura's site — content-forward, no decoration.

Pros

  • Maximum clarity
  • Closest to reference
  • Timeless, professional

Cons

  • Can feel plain
  • Less personality

B: Soft Minimal

Still very clean, but uses subtle card backgrounds (#fafafa) to group items. No divider lines. Section titles are regular weight, not uppercase. Slightly warmer feel.

Pros

  • More visual structure
  • Easier to scan
  • Bit more personality

Cons

  • Slightly less "pure" minimal
  • More elements on page

My recommendation: Approach A (Document Style). It's closest to what you described — maximum clean, text-first, straight to content. The white background and simple typography do the heavy lifting.
